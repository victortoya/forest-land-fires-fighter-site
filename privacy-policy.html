Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Forest Land Fires Fighter ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information on our own servers.

However, the App integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following data for advertising purposes:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device model, OS version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected only after you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline tracking, AdMob will serve non-personalised ads and limit data collection accordingly.

2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In compliance with Apple's requirements, the App will ask for your permission before allowing third-party advertising networks to track you across other apps and websites. You may change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. How We Use Information

We (and our advertising partner Google) use collected data to:

  • Display relevant advertisements within the App
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • Improve the App and user experience

We do not sell your personal data to third parties beyond what is described here.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

AdMob is operated by Google LLC. Its data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
Google Ad Technology: How Google uses data

5. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data on our own servers. Any data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own data retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly address the concern.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including:

  • Access to data collected about you
  • Deletion of your data
  • Opting out of personalised advertising (via ATT settings on your device)

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
