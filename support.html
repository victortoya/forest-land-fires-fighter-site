Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control the aircraft?

Tap and drag anywhere on the screen to steer the aircraft. The water bomber will follow your touch and automatically drop water on fires below it.

What is the goal of the game?

Extinguish as many forest fires as possible while avoiding the palm monsters. The longer you survive and the more fires you put out, the higher your score.

How do I unlock more aircraft?

New aircraft become available on the aircraft selection screen. Each aircraft has different handling and water capacity characteristics.

Why does the app ask for tracking permission?

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads, which may personalise ads based on your activity across other apps if you grant permission. You can decline and the app will still work normally — it will just show non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The app crashed or is not working correctly. What should I do?

Try the following steps:

  • Force-close the app and reopen it.
  • Restart your iPhone.
  • Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store.
  • If the problem persists, email us at toya.luckyrich@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

We love hearing from players! Send your feedback or bug reports to toya.luckyrich@gmail.com and we'll review every message.
